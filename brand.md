# Brand: AIMO

## Logo

- **Wordmark** (`docs/brand/aimo-wordmark.svg`): lowercase "aimo", drawn as monoline geometric strokes. The dot on the "a" is a memory node, and the violet dot on the "i" is the only accent. Use it on white or light surfaces at 20 px tall or more.
- **Mark** (`docs/brand/aimo-mark.svg`): the "a" glyph in white on a violet rounded square. Use it for favicons, avatars and app icons.
- Keep clear space equal to the height of the "a" on every side. Don't recolour, stretch, add effects or set the name in a typeface instead of using the SVG.

## Type

- **Geist** for all text: 600 for headings with tight tracking (-0.03 to -0.045em), 400–500 for body.
- **Geist Mono** for commands and numbers in code.

## Voice

Plain, calm and specific. Say what happens ("nothing in memory can spend money without your approval"), not how clever it is. Avoid jargon on public pages, put technical detail in the docs, and state limits openly.
